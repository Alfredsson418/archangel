# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in the Archangel project respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions, reviews, and collaboration.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack or demean people.
- Share relevant context, assume good faith where reasonable, and remain open to feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or private communications without permission.
- Keep project discussions focused and avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or unwanted sexual attention.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes targeted insults or threats, discriminatory or harassing language, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct after a request to stop.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub, for example by contacting the repository owner directly. Do not use a public issue or pull request for sensitive details. Include a concise description, relevant links or context, and whether there is an immediate safety concern. Share only information needed to review the report.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as practical, protect confidentiality to the extent possible, and may take proportionate action, including a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces. A person involved in a report should not be the sole decision-maker for that report.

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
